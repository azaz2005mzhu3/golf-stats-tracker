# Final Project Proposal: Golf Stats Tracker

## The three ideas

1. **Golf Stats Tracker:** A tool for me, Alice Zhu, or anyone that loves and plays golf, to record statistics after each golf round, compare rounds, and identify areas to practice.
2. **Study Session Planner:** A tool for me to organize assignments and exams and decide what to study based on deadlines and available time.
3. **Design Project Shortlist:** A tool for my sister, Ariel, to organize material options, prices, and links for interior design projects.

## Why not the other two

- **Study Session Planner:** Golf statistics give me a more specific problem to solve, and I can check the results using data from my own rounds.
- **Design Project Shortlist:** I would need to learn more about my sister’s current workflow and material data. For the golf tracker, I understand my own needs and can provide the data myself.

## What I am building

I am building a golf stats tracker that records round statistics, compares performance over time, and helps me decide what to practice.

## Who it is for

I am building this app for myself, Alice Zhu. After each golf round, I want to understand my performance beyond my total score. I want to track fairways hit, greens in regulation, total putts, and missed putts within five feet, so I can notice patterns across rounds.

## What the user does

After a round, I enter the date, course name, number of holes,
course par, total score, fairways hit and opportunities, greens
in regulation, total putts, and attempts and misses within five feet.

The app calculates my fairway percentage, GIR percentage,
average putts per hole, and short-putt make percentage.
I can save the round, view previous rounds, and compare my results.
